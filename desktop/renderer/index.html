Four-Tier YouTube Ad Handling Engine

The extension operates a prioritized defensive pipeline without breaking normal video playback.

01
Instant Native Skip (0ms)

Directly invokes movie_player.skipAd() in the page's execution context at the microsecond an ad begins.

02
Ad-End Seeking

If unskippable, executes movie_player.seekTo(video.duration, true) to conclude the ad stream.

03
Controlled 16x Fast-Forward & Audio Mute

Asserts 16x speed and mutes audio if seeking is buffered, finishing 15-second ads in <1 second.

04
Official Button Click & Clean State Restoration

Clicks official controls as fallback and restores the exact original playback speed (1.0x, 1.5x, 1.75x, 2.0x) and mute status.
